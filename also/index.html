How to spend a day

Choose activities and add them to your plan. Times fill in automatically, and the rest of your day adjusts when you add or remove something. Leave room for yourself and for Dad. Activities can move into later periods, but cannot overlap or go past midnight.

Morning care starts at 8am; night care ends at midnight. Missing required care affects Dad’s health. New care needs begin the following day. Unexpected emergencies can take priority when you end the day, pushing activities later or leaving some undone. End the day to see what happened, then continue.

Your progress saves in this browser. Export a save to keep a backup or move to another device. This is a fictional simulation, not medical guidance.
