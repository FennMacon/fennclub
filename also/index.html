How to spend a day

Choose activities and add them to your plan. Fill morning, then lunch, afternoon, evening and night. A full period moves you to the next one automatically; you can also leave time open. Times are arranged for you. Leave room for yourself and for Dad. Choose activities that fit the remaining time in each period.

Morning care starts at 8am; night care ends at midnight. Required care is reserved automatically and grows longer as Dad’s needs increase. New care needs begin the following day. When your plan is ready, play the day. Unexpected emergencies can take priority, pushing activities later or leaving some undone. Review what happened, then plan the next day.

Your progress saves in this browser. Export a save to keep a backup or move to another device. This is a fictional simulation, not medical guidance.
