=== CARNIVAL_COTTON_CANDY ===
NAME: Cotton Candy Stall

Fluffy pink and blue sugar spun around a paper cone. The smell hits you from ten feet away.

=== CARNIVAL_FRIED_DOUGH ===
NAME: Fried Dough Stall

Dough dropped in hot oil, dusted with powdered sugar. A Massachusetts carnival staple.

=== CARNIVAL_HOT_DOGS ===
NAME: Hot Dog Stand

Steamed dogs, yellow mustard, relish. Tastes better at a fair than anywhere else.

=== CARNIVAL_LEMONADE ===
NAME: Lemonade Stand

Fresh-squeezed lemonade in wax cups. Too sweet, too cold, exactly right.

=== CARNIVAL_FUNNEL_CAKE ===
NAME: Funnel Cake Booth

Crispy fried dough in a lacy spiral. Powdered sugar everywhere.

=== CARNIVAL_RING_TOSS ===
NAME: Ring Toss

Wooden pegs and rubber rings. Three tries for a prize.

=== CARNIVAL_BALLOON_DARTS ===
NAME: Balloon Darts

Colorful balloons pinned to a backboard. Three darts, a dollar.

=== CARNIVAL_BOTTLE_KNOCKDOWN ===
NAME: Bottle Knockdown

Milk bottles stacked in a pyramid. Throw a ball, knock them down.

=== CARNIVAL_FURY ===
NAME: Pharaoh’s Fury

Ten rows face the center of a gold-trimmed ship. Twin arms carry it higher with every pass, until the midway drops out from under the bow. The empty benches come back through with a rush of wind.

=== CARNIVAL_FREESTYLE ===
NAME: Freestyle

Twenty-four outward-facing seats line a bright circular rim. The turret turns one way, the rim the other, and the whole assembly leans into the sky. Shoulder bars wait above the empty seats.

=== CARNIVAL_WIPEOUT ===
NAME: Wipeout

Face-to-face benches circle a tilted platform. The spinning disk rolls through a slow wave, lifting one side while the other skims the loading deck. A faded surf motif promises a day at the beach.

=== CARNIVAL_CAROUSEL ===
NAME: Midway Carousel

Fourteen painted horses rise and fall beneath a striped roof. Brass poles catch the warm bulbs; a small chariot follows at the back. For a moment, the rest of the carnival feels far away.

=== CARNIVAL_YOYO ===
NAME: Yo-Yo

Thirty-two chain-hung chairs spread outward around a broad crown. The tower stays still while the canopy turns above it. Empty swings trace the same circle, over and over, without touching.

=== CARNIVAL_DUCK_POND ===
NAME: Duck Pond

Little yellow ducks crowd a shallow blue pool. Each has a number underneath, and the handwritten sign says every duck wins. The smallest prizes hang closest to the counter.

=== CARNIVAL_BASKETBALL ===
NAME: Hoop Shot

Three balls wait beneath a hoop that looks just a little too small. A row of stuffed bears watches from the rafters. You can almost hear the ball rattling around the rim.

=== CARNIVAL_SKEEBALL ===
NAME: Skee Ball

Two wooden lanes lead to rings of bright targets. The polished ramps remember thousands of attempts. Fifty points sits at the top, looking closer than it is.

=== CARNIVAL_TEACUPS ===
NAME: Twilight Teacups

Six pastel cups circle a teapot under warm bulbs. Each has a handwheel for adding your own spin, and a gap in the bowl where you climb inside. Even empty, they never quite agree on which way to turn.
